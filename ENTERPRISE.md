## Planned structure

Features aimed at organizations will live in a separate `enterprise/` directory and be covered by a
separate commercial license (`LICENSE-ENTERPRISE`), not by the Elastic License. Planned examples:

- Authentication, roles and audit log
- Hosted/managed compute and job scheduling
- Resource quotas and usage reporting
- Team workspaces and shared project galleries
- Priority support and SLAs

Nothing in `enterprise/` exists yet. Until it does, everything in this repository is under the Elastic License 2.0.
